Tutorial

Gesture guide
  1. Enable the camera, then arm gesture drawing.
  2. Touch thumb to pinky once to ready the pen.
  3. Move your index fingertip to draw.
  4. Touch thumb to pinky again to lift the pen.
  5. In Select, dwell to highlight, then pinch to drag.
